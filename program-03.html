India Launches New Weather Satellite

India has successfully launched a new weather satellite to improve weather forecasting and monitor changing climate conditions. The satellite will collect important information about clouds, rainfall, storms, and atmospheric conditions across the country.

The satellite was launched on 18 August 2026 from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota. The new satellite will provide valuable data for weather forecasting and disaster management. Scientists will use the information to improve predictions and provide timely warnings.

The new technology is expected to help authorities prepare for cyclones, floods, heavy rainfall, and other weather related events. Accurate weather information can help protect lives and reduce damage during natural disasters.

Experts believe that the satellite will also support farmers by providing useful information about rainfall and changing weather patterns. The data can help farmers make better decisions about irrigation and crop management.

Editor's Note

Weather satellites play an important role in modern forecasting. Better weather information can help people, farmers, and government agencies prepare for changing weather conditions.

Electric Vehicles Become More Popular in Indian Cities

Electric vehicles are becoming increasingly popular in Indian cities as more people look for cleaner and more economical transportation. New electric cars, scooters, and buses are being introduced in the market, giving customers more options.

Several cities are also developing charging stations to support the growing number of electric vehicles. Electric mobility is becoming an important part of discussions about the future of transportation.

Experts say that electric vehicles can help reduce pollution and dependence on traditional fuels. Expanding charging infrastructure will be important for the continued growth of electric transportation.

Many young consumers are showing interest in electric vehicles because of their lower running costs and environmentally friendly features. The shift towards cleaner transportation could play an important role in reducing urban pollution.

Editor's Note

The development of electric transportation is changing the way people think about daily travel. Continued investment in technology and charging infrastructure can make electric vehicles more convenient for everyone.
