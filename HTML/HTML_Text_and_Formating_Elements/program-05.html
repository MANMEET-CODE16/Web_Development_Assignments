Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is a chemical compound made up of hydrogen and oxygen. Its chemical formula is H2O.

Carbon dioxide has the chemical formula CO2. It is an important gas involved in photosynthesis and the carbon cycle.

Oxygen has the chemical formula O2. It is essential for respiration in humans and many other living organisms.

Sulfuric acid has the chemical formula H2SO4. It is a strong acid that is widely used in industries.

Hydrogen sulfide has the chemical formula H2S. It is a gas with a characteristic unpleasant smell.

Physics

Speed is the distance travelled by an object per unit of time. The SI unit of speed is metre per second, written as m/s.

The area of a square can be represented as m2.

The volume of a cube can be represented as m3.

The speed of light in vacuum is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Newton's Second Law is represented by the formula F = ma, where F is force, m is mass, and a is acceleration.

Biology

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare food using sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment found in plants that helps absorb light energy for photosynthesis.

The human body requires oxygen for cellular respiration. Oxygen helps cells release energy from food.

DNA stands for Deoxyribonucleic Acid. It contains genetic information that controls the characteristics and development of living organisms.
