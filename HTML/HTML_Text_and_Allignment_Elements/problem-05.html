Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter and its properties.

Water has the chemical formula H2O.

Carbon dioxide has the formula CO2.

Oxygen has the formula O2.

Hydrogen sulfide has the formula H2S.

Sulfuric acid has the formula H2SO4.

Definition: An element is a pure substance made of only one type of atom.

Physics

Area is measured in m2.

Volume is measured in m3.

Speed can be expressed as 3 x 108 m/s.

Critical Statement: Force is the product of mass and acceleration.

Biology

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food using sunlight.

Definition: A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

The human body contains approximately 1013 to 1014 cells.
