GLA UNIVERSITY NOIDA

# About My College:

GLA University was established nearly three decades ago with a clear focus on quality education, discipline, and employability. Over the last 28 years, GLA has evolved into one of North India's most trusted university brands, particularly known for its placement performance and strong industry relationships.


# Course Offered:

GLA University offers courses in various fields such as B.Tech, BBA, MBA, MCA, BCA, Law, Pharmacy, B.Com, B.Sc, B.A., M.Tech and M.Sc. At the Greater Noida campus, major programs include B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE with AI & ML, Electronics & Computer Engineering, AI-Enabled BBA, AI-Enabled MBA and MCA (AI & ML).


# Campus Life:

Campus life at GLA is lively and engaging, with opportunities to participate in sports, cultural events, clubs, competitions and extracurricular activities. Students can take part in activities such as music, dance, drama, cricket, athletics, chess and other events, helping them develop skills beyond academics


# Why I like my college:

I like my college because it provides a good balance between studies, practical learning and fun campus life. I enjoy the friendly environment, opportunities to participate in different activities, and the exposure to technology and real-world projects. Being in Greater Noida also gives me access to the opportunities and environment of the Delhi-NCR region.
