About GLA University

GLA University, Greater Noida, is a premier institution dedicated to academic excellence, innovation, and industry-oriented learning. Established to foster leadership and critical thinking, the university provides a vibrant environment where students can excel in various disciplines. With state-of-the-art infrastructure and highly experienced faculty, GLA University prepares graduates to meet global professional challenges.

Academic Programs

The university offers a diverse range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs tailored to current market demands. Students can pursue degrees in Engineering, Computer Applications, Management, Biotechnology, Humanities, and Applied Sciences. Every curriculum combines theoretical foundation with practical industry exposure to ensure comprehensive career readiness.

Campus Life

Life at GLA University extends beyond the classroom. The campus hosts numerous cultural clubs, sports facilities, tech fests, and leadership seminars that encourage extracurricular growth. Modern hostel facilities, well-equipped laboratories, and expansive green grounds create a supportive and engaging atmosphere for personal development.

Admissions

Admission to GLA University is based on academic performance, entrance exam scores, and personal interviews. Prospective students are encouraged to review eligibility criteria carefully prior to submitting their applications.

Important Message: All applicants must complete the online verification process before attending final counseling sessions.

Admission Deadline: Fall semester applications close strictly on July 31, 2026.
