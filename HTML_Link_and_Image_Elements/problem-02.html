Breaking News: Heavy Rain Brings Delhi-NCR to a Standstill

Red Alert Issued as Waterlogging and Flight Disruptions Hit the Capital

August 25, 2026 — New Delhi — Heavy rainfall once again lashed Delhi-NCR on Tuesday, causing severe waterlogging, traffic congestion and disruption to daily life. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) issued a red alert for Delhi, warning residents about further heavy rain, thunderstorms, lightning and gusty winds.

Several important areas of the capital, including Connaught Place, ITO, AIIMS, Parliament Street and RK Puram, reported significant waterlogging. Commuters faced long traffic jams as vehicles struggled to move through flooded roads. Some areas received around 100 mm of rainfall or more in the 24-hour period ending at 8:30 a.m.

The bad weather also affected air travel. On the previous day, 15 Delhi-bound flights were diverted because of adverse weather conditions, including international flights. Airlines including Air India, IndiGo and SpiceJet issued advisories asking passengers to check their flight status before travelling. Delhi Airport also warned passengers about the possibility of weather-related disruption.

The heavy rainfall has been linked to the interaction of weather systems affecting the region. The combination of intense showers, thunderstorms and strong winds created difficult conditions for commuters and airport operations. Authorities and civic agencies deployed teams to manage waterlogging and traffic, while Gurugram authorities advised offices and educational institutions to operate from home because of the severe weather conditions.
