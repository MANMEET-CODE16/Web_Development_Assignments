Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must carry their valid college identity card whenever they are on the college campus.

2. Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and other college facilities.

Students should respect teachers, staff members, visitors, and other students at all times. Respectful behaviour helps create a positive learning environment.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance in their classes.

Students should attend lectures regularly and arrive on time. Attendance below the required percentage may affect eligibility for examinations.

Examination

4. Students must carry their examination identity card and required stationery during examinations.

Students must follow all examination instructions and must not use unauthorized books, notes, mobile phones, or electronic devices. Any unfair practice may result in disciplinary action.

Students must report to the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled examination time.

Assignment Submission

5. Students must submit assignments before the deadline given by the respective teacher.

The standard submission deadline is 11:59 PM on the assigned due date. Late submissions may receive reduced marks.
Students should check the instructions carefully before submitting their assignments.

Assignments must contain the student's correct name, roll number, course name, and submission details. Students should keep a copy of their submitted work for future reference.

Discipline

6. Students must not participate in bullying, harassment, violence, or any behaviour that creates an unsafe environment.

Students must use college property responsibly and must not damage classrooms, furniture, laboratory equipment, or other facilities. Damage to college property may result in a penalty.

Students should use mobile phones responsibly during college hours. Mobile phones should be kept silent during lectures and examinations.

Important Notice

All students are expected to read and follow these rules. Failure to follow the rules may result in warnings, loss of academic privileges, or other disciplinary action according to institutional policies.
