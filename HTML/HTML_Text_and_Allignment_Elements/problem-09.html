Student Terms and Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their identity card to college every day.

Students must follow the instructions given by teachers and college authorities.

These rules are made to maintain a safe and organized learning environment.

Attendance

Students must maintain at least 75 percent attendance.

Students must inform the teacher in case of absence.

Attendance shortage may affect eligibility for examinations.

Examination

Students must carry their examination identity card.

Students must reach the examination hall before the reporting time.

Students must not use unfair means during examinations.

Any student found using unfair means may face disciplinary action.

Assignment Submission

Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.

Assignments must be submitted in the required format.

Assignments submitted after the deadline may receive a penalty.

For example, an assignment due on Monday must be submitted on or before Monday.

Discipline

Students must maintain discipline inside and outside the classroom.

Students must respect teachers, staff members, and fellow students.

Students must not damage college property.

Serious violations may result in disciplinary action according to college rules.

Important Notice

All students are required to follow these rules.

Failure to follow the rules may result in penalties or disciplinary action.

Students should contact the college office if they need clarification about any rule.
